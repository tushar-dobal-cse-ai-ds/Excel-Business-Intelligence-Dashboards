# 👨‍💼 HR Analytics Dashboard (Microsoft Excel)

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze workforce data and employee attrition. It enables HR professionals and business leaders to monitor key workforce metrics, identify attrition trends, and support data-driven decision-making through dynamic reporting.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to identify the factors contributing to employee attrition and workforce performance. This dashboard consolidates HR data into an interactive reporting solution, allowing stakeholders to monitor workforce metrics, explore trends, and identify areas requiring attention.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition
- Monitor workforce demographics
- Compare department-wise performance
- Analyze salary distribution
- Evaluate overtime impact on attrition
- Support HR decision-making through interactive reporting

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

Dataset includes:

- Employee Information
- Department
- Job Role
- Education
- Age
- Gender
- Monthly Income
- Years at Company
- Overtime
- Attrition Status
